◆ drag_max

char meta::keys::ui::drag_max[] = "ui.drag_max"
inlineconstexpr

Highest value the rail spans, when that is lower than the real max.

A rail has to end somewhere comfortable to drag across, but the parameter behind it may legitimately accept far more. Spatial Frequency is the case that prompted this: 0 to 64 is the useful range to aim at, yet a user who knows they want 512 should be able to type it.

Absent, the rail spans to constraints::max and typing is bounded by the same value, which is the behaviour every attribute had before this key existed.
